This research involves the development of data analysis and natural language processing (NLP) techniques to solve practical issues, such as creating methods for accurately predicting product quality and developing document-based approaches for proactive problem prevention.

## Topics

### Discovery of constitutive models

Sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy) applied to rheological data of complex fluids, which yields interpretable physical models from measured data.

![Constitutive model discovery with SINDy](/images/research/rheo_sindy.svg)

### Pharmaceutical continuous manufacturing

Setpoint determination based on residence time distribution, soft sensors for tablet quality attributes, and transfer learning from batch to continuous manufacturing.

### Transfer learning for process data

Methods based on generative adversarial networks (GANs) for regression problems and chemical reaction processes.

### Medical and healthcare data

Modification of R-R interval (RRI) data, generation of disease-specific electrocardiogram (ECG) data, prediction of postoperative delirium, and prediction of postprandial blood glucose levels.
